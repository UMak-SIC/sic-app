Tab 08 // System & Administration

System Governance & Retention Automation Flow

Automatic attendance closing two hours after each event ends, five-year data archiving, and single-administrator access.

1 26. System Governance & 5-Year Retention
26. System settings Step 1 Retention and event-close automation
UMak SIC
OverviewEventsAttendeesCampaignsAssetsSettings
Charles ReyesAdministrator

System settings

Organisation-wide attendance and retention rules.

Event close automation
Check-in is accepted from two hours before start through two hours after end. The event closes two hours after the end, then everyone still waiting is marked absent.
Five-year retention
At five years, student names, student numbers, and email addresses are replaced with anonymous labels, attendance times are rounded, and campaign and delivery content is deleted. Each clean-up saves a record of what it did.
Asset retention
Uploaded files are removed with expired campaigns and events. A file still in use cannot be deleted.
Administration
This application has one authorised administrator. There is no self sign-up, no separate roles, and no way to add a second administrator.
