18. System settingsRetention and event-close automation
UMak SIC
OverviewEventsAttendeesCampaignsAssets
Clerk-backed adminSingle authorised account

System settings

Organisation-wide attendance and retention rules.

Event close automation
Check-in is accepted from two hours before start through two hours after end. The event closes at ends_at + 2 hours, then pending roster entries become absent.
Five-year retention
At five years, attendee identifiers are anonymized, campaign and delivery content is deleted, attendance timestamps are anonymized, and a retention job records completion or failure.
Asset retention
R2 originals are deleted with expired campaign or event records. Referenced assets remain protected until their references are eligible for deletion.
Administration
This application has one Clerk-backed authorised admin. There is no self-registration, role management, or multiple-admin UI.
